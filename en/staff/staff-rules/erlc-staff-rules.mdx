  # A. General Section
</div>

### A-1.1 — Server Leak / Sabotage

Deliberately causing harm to the server through massive, conscious, or intentional violations of the staff regulations, as well as by any other means.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Permanent ban on the game server, on Discord servers + entry into the staff blacklist.
</Danger>

### A-1.2 — Sabotaging Players and Staff

Deliberately targeting/harassing players or staff members, focused targeting, as well as abuse of the report/ticket system for biased persecution. The punishment is issued for documenting a deliberate "hunt" on a player or another staff member, even if the submitted complaints have actual grounds.

<Warning>
  **_Note:_** This rule also applies to collusion. If a group of individuals (including players) coordinates and distributes complaints (for example, each uploads one ticket against the same target with the common intent to sabotage the person), all collusion participants will be punished, and their complaints will be annulled.
</Warning>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Strike for 3 months.
</Danger>

### A-1.3 — Interference in the RP Process

Staff members are strictly prohibited from interfering in the players' RP process.

<Danger>
  **Important exception:** Interference is only allowed if players are committing **Mass RDM** or in other extreme cases where rule violations exceed all limits.
</Danger>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** First a verbal warning for 1 week, then a warning for 1 week.
</Danger>

### A-1.4 — Interference in Other's Reports/Complaints

It is prohibited to interfere in complaints/reports of other staff members without their permission, both in-game and in Discord tickets. Any word written off-topic regarding complaints of other staff members will be considered a violation of this rule.

<Note>
  **Allowed in cases:**

  - If the staff member explicitly requested it in the staff chat.
  - Team Leads or management may interfere if they notice a fatal error in the review.
  - **_Note:_** Arbitrary "addition" to another's complaint with one's own evidence without the direct request of the reviewing staff member is strictly prohibited and equates to interference.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** First a verbal warning for 1 week, then a warning for 2 weeks.
</Danger>

### A-1.5 — Using Staff Capabilities Off-Duty

It is prohibited to use staff capabilities while off-duty.

<Info>
  **Allowed in cases:**

  - Emergencies: **Mass RDM**, **foreign players**, or any other emergency situation.
  - Management members may use commands off-duty on a reasonable scale, but only to assist players.
</Info>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** First a verbal warning for 2 weeks, then a warning for 1 month.
</Danger>

### A-1.6 — Unauthorized Events

It is prohibited to host events unless you are an Event Maker.

<Note>
  **_Note:_** ER:LC Staff is allowed to host RP situations for players, but strictly with prior approval from senior staff.
</Note>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Verbal warning for 2 weeks.
</Danger>

### A-1.7 — Negligence and Inappropriate Behavior

Negligent or inappropriate behavior while on duty is prohibited.

<Tip>
  **_Examples:_** A staff member gets personal and starts insulting, or simply ignores player violations.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 2 weeks.
</Danger>

### A-1.8 — Threats of Punishment

It is prohibited to use punishments or threats of issuing them for personal purposes, to intimidate players, or during personal conflicts.

<Note>
  **_Note:_** An official verbal warning does not constitute intimidation.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **_For example:_** "Player, stop the NonRP behavior, otherwise I will be forced to issue you a demorgan."
</Tip>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Strike for 1 month.
</Danger>

### A-1.9 — Ignoring Orders of Management

It is prohibited to intentionally ignore directives from a higher-ranking staff member, senior, or executive staff.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 2 weeks.
</Danger>

### A-1.10 — Reviewing Staff Complaints

Regular staff members are prohibited from reviewing complaints about violations by staff.

<Tip>
  - **Important:** Such complaints can only be reviewed by staff team leads and executive staff.
  - **Review hierarchy:** Complaints against team leads are reviewed by management, and complaints against management by project owners/administration.
</Tip>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 month.
</Danger>

### A-1.11 — Conflict of Interest

It is prohibited to review complaints involving yourself if you participated with the player in the same RP scene.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 month.
</Danger>

### A-2.1 — Mandatory Recording

Every staff member is required to record their screen with audio while performing their duties. The recording is necessary to confirm the validity of punishments and resolve disputes. The recording must be stored for at least 3 days.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 2 weeks.
</Danger>

### A-2.2 — Favoritism and Abuse of Privileges

It is prohibited to exceed the authority of your granted privileges, cover for friends, or interfere in punishments and complaints related to your friends.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 month.
</Danger>

### A-2.3 — Proportionality of Punishment

The punishment must be proportional to the committed violation.

<Info>
  **Rules for determining duration:**

  - **Note 1:** The staff member must consider all circumstances, goals, and motives of the player's violation to issue a fair punishment duration.
  - **Note 2:** The maximum punishment duration is issued only if the player has grossly violated one or more server rules.
</Info>

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 week.
</Danger>

### A-2.4 — Unjustified Removal of Punishments

It is prohibited to lift restrictions from a player unless there is a justified reason, a ticket decision, or a directive from senior staff.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 month.
</Danger>
 
### A-2.5 — Disclosure of Internal Information

It is strictly prohibited to disclose internal information, correspondence, decisions of the staff, as well as to broadcast any internal chats (inaccessible to ordinary players) on screen shares, streams, publish their photos, screenshots, or distribute them in any other way.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 3 months or termination.
</Danger>

### A-2.6 — Disclosure of Confidential Information

If the project leadership or management has instructed not to disclose certain information (whether it is plans for future updates, upcoming personnel changes, internal investigations, technical details, or any other information), staff members are strictly prohibited from sharing or distributing it in any form.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Strike for 1 month or termination.
</Danger>

### B-1.2 — Reporting on Commands Off-Duty

Any use of staff commands off-duty requires a mandatory report via the `/отчёт команды` command.

**The report must specify:**

- Which commands were used.
- Reason for use.
- Time of command application.
- Link to the command log (from the **”📑┃история-команд”** channel).

<Note>
  It is allowed to combine several commands into one report if they were applied within the same situation and around the same time. These rules apply even in emergency situations (raids, mass violations) — after the threat is resolved, you are required to document all punishments strictly according to the regulations.
</Note>

### C-1.1 — Disciplinary System and Limits

The server implements a strict division of punishments based on the severity of the misconduct:

- **Verbal warning** — issued for a fixed term for minor violations.
- **Official warning (Warn)** — issued for significant misconduct. Every **3 warns** automatically convert into **1 strike**.
- **Strike** — a critical measure. Strict limits apply:
  - **2 strikes** — temporary suspension from duty, initiation of an investigation, and possible demotion.
  - **3 strikes** — automatic termination from the staff.

### C-1.2 — Order of Issuing Punishments

<Note>
  Accumulation of disciplinary punishments occurs strictly according to a single unified algorithm:
</Note>

1. **\[Verbal Warning\]** ⟶ Initial stage for minor misconduct.
2. **\[Warn #1\]** ⟶ **\[Warn #2\]** ⟶ **\[Warn #3\]**
   - Automatic conversion: **(STRIKE #1)**
3. **\[Warn #1\]** ⟶ **\[Warn #2\]** ⟶ **\[Warn #3\]**
   - Automatic conversion: **(STRIKE #2)** ⟶ `[Temporary suspension + internal investigation]`
4. **(STRIKE #3)** ⟶ ⚠️ **\[AUTOMATIC TERMINATION FROM STAFF]**

### C-1.3 — Emergency Situation Rules

- **Instant strike:** Management has the right to issue a strike immediately, bypassing the warning stage, if the staff member's misconduct is deemed gross.
- **Instant termination:** A staff member is terminated without discussion for: disrespect to the owner, abuse of power, sabotage, or causing critical damage to the project.
- **Registration:** All warns and strikes must be recorded in a special log channel.
- **Investigation:** Investigations and audits within the staff are conducted exclusively by the Internal Affairs Team and the Staff Manager.

### C-1.4 — Step-by-Step Algorithm for Resolving Misconduct

The disciplinary regulations are mandatory for everyone, including management. Any incident goes strictly through four stages:

1. **Recording:** The fact of misconduct is recorded by the log system or other staff.
2. **Explanatory statement:** The staff member is required to provide written explanations in a duty ticket to defend their position. Deadline: **48 hours** (if the staff member is in-game — **4 hours**). Refusal equals admission of guilt.
3. **Review:** The staff manager or Internal Affairs Team reviews the arguments and renders a verdict.
4. **Penalty:** Official notification (penalty order) specifying the reason, term, and type of disciplinary action.

### D-1.1 — Distribution Standards for Various Player Counts

The number of staff on shift and their positioning strictly depend on the current number of players on the server.

<Info>
  **Summary table of staff balance:**

  | Player Online | Min Staff | Max Staff | Mandatory Map Distribution |
  | :-- | :-: | :-: | :-- |
  | **2 – 10 players** | 1 | 2 | 1 staff member stands at spawn with their duty transport. |
  | **10 – 15 players** | 1 | 3 | 1 staff member stands at spawn with their duty transport. |
  | **15 – 20 players** | 2 – 3 | 4 | 2 staff members stand at spawn. The 3rd is located in a place of high activity (e.g., MVD building). |
  | **20 – 30 players** | 3 – 5 | 5 | 2 staff members stand at spawn. The rest are distributed among activity points (MVD, hospital, EMERCOM, parking lots). |
  | **30 – 39 players** | 5+ | 6 | 2 staff members remain at spawn. The rest are located near spawn and in places of high activity. |

  <Tip>
    _Note:_ If the server has a minimal player count (e.g., 5 players), it is highly discouraged for two or more staff members to go on duty simultaneously.
  </Tip>
</Info>

### D-1.2 — Staff Placement Points

Fixed points are set for staff to stay in standby mode (spawn, MVD, hospital, EMERCOM, large public places).

- **Point limit:** No more than two staff representatives may be at one point simultaneously.
- **Grouping prohibition:** It is prohibited for more than three staff members to stand together. Staff must be evenly distributed across the map and not gather in one place without duty necessity.

### D-1.3 — City Patrols and Response

Every active staff member is required to maintain dynamic control over the situation on the server.

- **Frequency:** In the absence of urgent requests (tickets/mod calls), the staff member must go on a city patrol every **5–10 minutes**.
- **Patrol procedure:** Patrol is carried out in duty transport through the city and problem areas, after which the staff member returns to their position.

**Response to mod-calls:** During patrol, the staff member must promptly accept mod calls if they are closest to the incident site. After resolving the call, they must continue the patrol or return to the point.

<Note>
  **Important restriction:** Staff is strictly prohibited from constantly staying AFK in one place and limiting themselves solely to lazy replies to mod calls from the menu.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you have gone out on patrol, don't forget to turn on the 2nd stage on the siren.
</Tip>

### D-1.4 — Violation of Staff Balance

Ignoring patrols, constant groundless grouping of three or more staff members in one point, or refusing to distribute across the map is considered a violation of discipline.

<Danger>
  **_Punishment:_** Warning for 1 week for violating the balance.
</Danger>
