  # Staff Rights
</div>

<Note>
  **Important notice on rights flexibility (delegation and restrictions):**
  The higher project management (Owner, Co-Owner, Project Leadership, and authorized Managers) reserves the right to temporarily extend or restrict the permissions of lower-ranking staff members within reasonable limits to perform specific work tasks.

  **_Example:_** A Factions Manager may be temporarily granted the authority of a Community Manager during the latter's absence to perform urgent tasks.

  **Warning:** The existence of this possibility does not grant the right to independently ask, beg, or demand additional permissions. Any unjustified requests for permission expansion (including those referencing this note) are strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.
</Note>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="F-1 — Owner">
    - **F-1.1 — Permissions:**
      - Absolute authority and final decisions on all matters of the project, server, and staff.
      - Can appoint, demote, promote, and terminate any staff member.
      - Can revoke any decisions and modify the staff structure.
      - Delegates authority to the Co-Owner.
      - No limitations on rights.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-2 — Co-Owner">
    - **F-2.1 — Permissions:**
      - All rights of the Owner, except for the ability to remove or replace the Owner.
      - Substitutes for the Owner in all matters during their absence.
      - Manages staff, assets/technical equipment, rules, and approves HR decisions.
    - **F-2.2 — Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to remove or limit the Owner's rights.
      - It is prohibited to make changes to the rules without agreement from the Owner.
      - It is prohibited to disclose confidential information (NDA): update plans, personal data of staff, contents of internal meetings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F-3 — Project Leadership">
    - **F-3.1 — Permissions:**
      - Full management of the project and server, and full responsibility for all its aspects.
      - Can manage staff, rules, technical features, and gameplay.
      - Can appoint, demote, promote, and terminate subordinate staff members.
    - **F-3.2 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - Subject to demotion, suspension, or removal by the Owner or Co-Owner.
      - It is prohibited to remove, limit, or modify the rights of the Owner or Co-Owner.
      - It is prohibited to disclose confidential information (NDA): update plans, personal data of staff, contents of internal meetings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="F-4 — Head Developer">
    - **F-4.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Executing technical tasks and assignments received from management (Owner, Co-Owner, Project Leadership).
    - **F-4.2 — Permissions:**
      - Exercise operational leadership over the development team.
      - Coordinate the process of executing tasks set by the Owner, Co-Owner, or Project Leadership of the project.
      - Distribute current workload among members of the technical department to optimize workflow.
      - Control the quality and deadlines of executing technical assignments received from management.
    - **F-4.3 — Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to interfere in HR processes without the consent of the Owner/Co-Owner/Project Leadership.
      - It is prohibited to interfere in the operations of other departments (staff, event management, factions) without direct permission from the Owner (in case of events — only upon approval of the Community Managers to assist).
      - It is prohibited to use the position to gain personal advantages in gameplay or lobby the interests of specific players/factions.
      - It is prohibited to disclose confidential information (NDA): update plans, personal data of staff, contents of internal meetings.
      - Any actions leading to the destabilization of team operations or causing reputational/technical harm to the server.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-5 — Staff Manager">
    - **F-5.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Organizing training, conducting recruitment, supervising internships and mentorship systems for Team Leads, Administrators, and Moderators.
      - Control of the Internal Affairs Team.
      - Control of the Discord Moderation Team.
      - Regularly conducting evaluations, knowledge testing (tests), and deciding on rotation (promotion/demotion/transfer) of staff.
      - Making final decisions on hiring and termination.
    - **F-5.2 — Permissions:**
      - Suspend decisions of subordinate administrators if they contradict the rules or interests of the project.
      - Request work reports from Team Leads and moderators at any time.
      - Suspend any staff member from duty during an internal investigation without prior agreement from management.
      - Nominate outstanding staff members for bonuses or unique roles.
      - Amend staff instructions and the code of ethics (in coordination with the Owner).
    - **F-5.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to interfere in the operations of other departments (technical part, event management, factions) without direct permission from the Owner (in case of events — only upon approval of the Community Managers to assist).
      - It is prohibited to use the position to gain personal advantages in gameplay or lobby the interests of specific players/factions.
      - It is prohibited to disclose confidential information (NDA): update plans, personal data of staff, contents of internal meetings.
      - Any actions leading to the destabilization of team operations or causing reputational/technical harm to the server.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-6 — Community Manager">
    - **F-6.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Planning and conducting in-game events, creative contests, and giveaways. Coordinating the work of Event Makers.
      - Managing social media, writing texts for update announcements, publishing news and PR materials.
      - Searching for bloggers, negotiating cooperation, and monitoring their execution of advertising obligations.
      - Monitoring Discord and social media. Conducting surveys, analyzing player sentiment, and preparing reports for the Owner.
      - Organizing live streams or Q&A sessions between players and staff/developers.
      - Developing initiatives to improve the atmosphere and interaction within the community and external audience.
      - Managing the hierarchy "Community Manager -> PR Leader -> Event Makers/Media Partners/SMM Specialists". Setting tasks to find new advertising platforms.
      - Supervising the promotion team (Media Partners/Event Makers/SMM Specialists). Creating technical specifications for advertising creatives, previews, and server trailers.
      - Tracking where new players come from (via promo codes or metrics) and adjusting promotion strategy.
      - Monitoring how the PR Leader and media faces of the project communicate with the audience on external resources.
    - **F-6.2 — Permissions:**
      - Access to resources: Right to request funds from the project fund for giveaways (in-game currency, donate, unique items).
      - Content moderation: Right to edit any information channels, delete outdated news, and format Discord/forum sections.
      - May be granted the right to give orders to moderators and administrators in the course of preparing events or advertising campaigns.
      - Right to officially respond on behalf of the project in public spaces.
      - Right to initiate blocking of users or media partners for causing reputational damage to the project.
      - Ability to reject a media partner (blogger) candidate proposed by the PR Leader if their content does not match the image of Moscow RolePlay.
      - Right of final approval of all visual and text materials used in advertising.
      - Right to remove the PR Leader and hire a new one.
    - **F-6.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to interfere in the operations of other departments (technical part, staff, factions) without direct permission from the Owner/Co-Owner/Project Leadership.
      - Strictly prohibited to use server resources to advertise personal projects, social networks, or external platforms not related to Moscow RolePlay.
      - It is prohibited to engage in public conflicts with players, use profane language, or show aggression on behalf of the project.
      - It is prohibited to disclose update details ahead of the scheduled time ("leaking" insider info).
      - It is prohibited to make deals with bloggers or partners on terms that imply personal benefit for the manager (kickbacks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-7 — Factions Manager">
    - **F-7.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Forming the curator team, distributing them among factions, and monitoring their daily activity.
      - Coordinating the actions of the Head Curator, setting weekly tasks, and verifying execution of directives.
      - Regular auditing of curators' actions for "nepotism/favoritism" towards their leaders.
      - Intervening in disputes that curators or the Head Curator could not resolve.
      - Reviewing appeals against decisions of the Head Curator or complaints about unlawful actions of curators.
      - Defining the overall policy for all factions (e.g., "month of emphasis on RP component" or "activation of the criminal sector").
    - **F-7.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to appoint, remove, or transfer curators from one faction to another to prevent stagnation.
      - Full access to curators' internal chats with leaders to monitor management quality.
      - Right to issue strikes to curators and the Head Curator for negligence or poor performance of their supervised factions.
      - Right to annul any decision of a curator or the Head Curator if it contradicts server rules or project interests.
      - Right to demand summary analytics from the Head Curator on all organizations for any period.
    - **F-7.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to issue orders to leaders directly, bypassing their curators (except for emergencies when the curator is offline).
      - It is prohibited to curate factions independently or have close personal connections with leaders/curators (nepotism).
      - It is prohibited to change internal rules of a specific faction without prior discussion with its curator and the Head Curator.
      - It is prohibited to transfer information about audits or raids to curators of other branches (e.g., informing the OPG curator about the plans of the MVD curator).
      - It is prohibited to interfere in the operations of other departments (technical part, event management, staff) without direct permission from the Owner (in case of events — only upon approval of the Community Managers to assist).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="F-8 — Roblox Internal Affairs Team">
    - **F-8.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Autonomous staff subdivision carrying out control over regulations and ethics compliance.
      - Conducting independent internal audits and investigations regarding violations by Roblox staff members.
      - Processing complaints from players and staff against staff members, maintaining the violations registry.
    - **F-8.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to request logs, screenshots, and other evidence from staff members necessary for an active investigation.
      - Right to recommend HR decisions (demotion, termination) to the management based on investigation results.
      - Right to temporarily suspend a staff member's administrative privileges during the investigation period (with prior coordination with the Staff Manager).
    - **F-8.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to make final HR decisions (termination, blacklisting, permanent bans) in a unilateral manner.
      - It is prohibited to conduct investigations against friends, members of one's game faction, or direct supervisors.
      - Strictly prohibited to use IAT authority to pressure staff members or for personal gain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-9 — Discord Head Moderator">
    - **F-9.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Leadership and coordination of Discord moderators, maintaining order on project communication servers.
      - Conducting recruitment, internships, and training for Discord moderators.
      - Reviewing complaints about actions of Discord moderators and resolving disputes in official channels.
    - **F-9.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to issue punishments (mutes, bans, warns) on the Discord server in accordance with the communication rules.
      - Right to configure channels, assign/remove Discord moderator roles (within their department).
      - Right to propose candidates for promotion or demotion among Discord moderators to the Staff Manager.
    - **F-9.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to punish game staff or interfere in game processes without coordination with the Staff Manager.
      - It is prohibited to grant access to closed or confidential channels to unauthorized individuals.
      - It is prohibited to use moderation features and assign roles for personal gain or to promote external resources.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-10 — PR Leader">

      - Monitoring activity and reputation of the project's media faces in the media space.
    - **F-10.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to propose candidates to the Community Manager for long-term partnerships or buying ads.
      - Right to request view and traffic statistics from bloggers for reports to management.
      - Right to issue instructions to the project's media partners within the framework of complying with advertising TS.
    - **F-10.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to make deals and sign advertising contracts on terms that cause harm to the project or imply personal benefit.
      - It is prohibited to disclose confidential details of upcoming updates (NDA) to bloggers or external parties without Community Manager approval.

      - Reviewing complaints against ER:LC administrators and moderators, reviewing unban appeals.

  <Accordion title="F-13 — Administrator ER:LC">
    - **F-13.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Maintaining order on the server, suppressing disruptive behavior.
      - Right to issue punishments to staff members and administrators lower in rank.
      - Reviewing complaints against lower-ranking ER:LC administrators and moderators.
      - Reviewing unban appeals.
    - **F-13.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to issue player punishments (kick, mute, jail, warn, ban, unban) in accordance with server rules.
      - Right to interfere in RP situations only during gross violations of the rules.
      - Right to approve mini- or medium-scale events, such as roadwork and hostage taking.
    - **F-13.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to groundlessly interfere in the work of junior administrators and moderators.
      - It is prohibited to make HR decisions regarding other staff members.
      - It is prohibited to use any administrative commands for personal gain or to help friends.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-14 — Junior Administrator ER:LC">
    - **F-14.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Assisting players on the server, resolving complaints and medium-complexity tickets, recording player violations, and maintaining discipline.
      - Right to issue punishments to staff members lower in rank.
      - Reviewing complaints against lower-ranking ER:LC moderators.
      - Reviewing unban appeals.
    - **F-14.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to use game punishment commands (kick, mute, warn, jail, ban) per server rules.
      - Right to temporarily interfere in game situations to stop mass rule violations (Mass RDM, Leave RP).
      - Right to approve mini- or medium-scale events, such as roadwork and hostage taking.
    - **F-14.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to make HR decisions or conduct staff audits.
      - It is prohibited to issue punishments without capturing evidence.
      - It is prohibited to use admin commands for personal purposes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="F-15 — Administration ER:LC">
    - **F-15.1 — Core Duties and Functions:**
      - Performing basic administrative duties, resolving simple complaints and tickets, helping players, and recording violations.
    - **F-15.2 — Permissions:**
      - Right to issue warnings and basic punishments (kick, mute, warn, jail) per server regulations.
      - Right to approve mini- or medium-scale events, such as roadwork and hostage taking.
    - **F-15.3 — Restrictions and Prohibitions:**
      - It is prohibited to issue global punishments (permanent bans) without prior coordination with higher-ranking Administrators.
      - It is prohibited to make HR decisions and use commands for personal gain.
