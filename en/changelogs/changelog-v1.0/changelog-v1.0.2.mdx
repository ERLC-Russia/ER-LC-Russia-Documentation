<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Department Oversight Bot Update">
    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-10"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>Department Oversight Update</strong></h3></div>

    ### Shift Tracking System — `/shift`
    * **Personal Panel:** `/shift manage` opens an interactive card with real-time status and action buttons: **Start**, **Break**, **End**.
    * **Shift Types:** Choose shift type on duty start (with dedicated duty roles, logs, and prefixes).
    * **Break Accounting:** Time spent on break is automatically excluded from total worked hours.
    * **Statistics & Quotas:** `/shift time` shows worked hours, average shift duration, and quota completion status considering role-specific quotas.
    * **Leaderboards & Reports:** `/shift leaderboard` with top-3 medals and text file export, `/shift history @employee`, `/shift activity` for any timeframe with leave and reduced activity status markers.
    * **Administration:** `/shift admin @employee` — force start/end shifts, manual time adjustments (“1h 30m”), voids, and cleanup.
    * **Staff Limits:** Configurable maximum number of concurrent personnel on shift.

    ---

    ### Leaves and Reduced Activity
    * **Submitting Applications:** `/loa submit` and `/ra submit` with flexible duration (1 to 90 days) and reason.
    * **Discord Review:** Application cards with employee service history drop into leadership review channels with **Approve** / **Deny** buttons (with mandatory reason).
    * **Role Automation:** Leave and reduced activity roles assigned upon approval and automatically removed every 5 minutes when expired; notifications sent via DM.
    * **Shift Integration:** Duty shifts blocked during active leaves; hour quotas reduced by 50% during reduced activity.
    * **Management:** `/loa admin` and `/ra admin` for manual grant, extension, and early termination.

    ---

    ### DM Notification Consent — `/consent`
    * Toggle for direct message deliveries from the bot (shift summaries, disciplinary notifications, and personnel orders).

    ---

    ### Unified Personnel Management Panel — `/manage`
    * Interactive employee profile card `/manage @employee` replacing scattered commands:
      * **Hire** into department with rank and prefix selection;
      * **Promote / Demote** with reason logs;
      * **Transfer** between divisions or factions;
      * **Dismiss** (standard resignation or blacklist with custom duration / permanent);
      * **Prefix Management** and early blacklist removal.
    * Automatic order numbering (№00000001), publishing to log channels, and DM notifications.

    ---

    ### Punishments and Suspensions — `/punishment`
    * Panel `/punishment @employee` for disciplinary enforcement.
    * **Escalation Levels:** Customizable violation types linked to tier roles (Level 1, 2, 3...).
    * **Automation:** Rules like “3 warnings → 1 reprimand for 1 month”.
    * **Auto-Expiration:** Automatic role removal when punishment terms expire.
    * **Suspensions:** Assigns suspension role, delivers explanatory statement form in DM (48 hours to reply, text + up to 5 files), and posts to review channel.

    ---

    ### Full Configuration from Discord — `/configure`
    * Interactive setup panel featuring 13 sections:
      1. **Language** — Russian or English server-wide;
      2. **Access Permissions** — config roles and management roles;
      3. **Rank System** — departments, ranks, dismissal types, blacklists, prohibited roles;
      4. **Punishments** — types, levels, automation, and suspensions;
      5. **Log Channels** — up to 25 audit and channel move log channels;
      6. **Google Forms** — integration and channel routing;
      7. **Global Ban** — partner server network;
      8. **Announcements** — broadcast department channels;
      9. **Modcalls** — dispatch channel, moderator roles, and fallback role;
      10. **Tickets** — categories, support roles, panel builder;
      11. **Shifts** — types, prefixes, quotas, limits;
      12. **Leaves** — review channel and leave roles;
      13. **Reduced Activity** — review channel and reduced activity roles.

    ---

    ### Server Data Cleanup — `/server manage`
    * Secure reset panel: shift history, leaves, reduced activity, ranks, and departments.
    * Safeguarded with mandatory confirmation phrase `Я ГОТОВ УДАЛИТЬ ДАННЫЕ` and graceful active shift termination.

    ---

    ### Emergency Moderation Calls — Modcalls
    * Command `/mod reason` via Discord and `:mod reason` directly inside ER:LC in-game chat.
    * Hub server alerts, one-click claim button, and automatic in-game moderator teleportation to the player in Roblox.
    * Admin panel `/modcall` with Discord/Roblox blacklists and emergency call lock.

    ---

    ### Global Ban and Announcements
    * **Global Ban:** `/global-ban` and `/global-unban` across all linked network servers simultaneously.
    * **Mass Announcements:** `/announcement for departments` with image URL and role ping support.
    * **Bilingual Support:** Complete localization in Russian and English.
