<div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full text-center">
  We carried out a large-scale update of server rules, faction rules, and staff regulations. Provocation and respawn rules have been clarified, multi-faction norms adjusted, and a completely new ER:LC Staff Regulations document has been introduced.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Server Rules Update">
    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-10"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>A. General Rules</strong></h3></div>

    * **A-8** — Provocation rules clarified:
      * **<span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Old version:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>If a violation occurred due to provocation by someone, the violation will not count, and the one who provoked will be punished.</span>
      * **<span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>New version:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>It is forbidden to give in to OOC provocations and justify server rule violations with IC reasons. If players gave in to a provocation and violated the rules (engaged in a shootout, NRP, OOC insults, etc.), the situation is not rolled back, and both parties receive punishment.</span>
        <Callout icon="circle-info" iconType="regular" color="#1a9fff">
          <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>**Note:** Lawful procedural actions by government structure employees (document checks, detentions, searches, etc.) are a full IC process and are not considered OOC provocation.</span>
        </Callout>
        <Callout icon="triangle-exclamation" iconType="regular" color="#ff4444">
          <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>**Important:** If a player attempted to provoke a violation but the other side did not give in and filed a complaint — only the provocateur receives punishment.</span>
        </Callout>

    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-14"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>E. Special Rules</strong></h3></div>

    * **E-2** — Respawn rules clarified:
      * **<span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Old version:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Allow the "Call EMS" call to be processed before respawning, wait 2 minutes if rescue services are not on the server.</span>
      * **<span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>New version:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>Upon serious injury or death (when the respawn banner appears), it is forbidden to respawn immediately:</span>
        * <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>If medics are on the server — you must call an ambulance and wait 5 minutes. If medics did not arrive in time, respawn is allowed; if they arrived — continue RP.</span>
        * <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>If there are no medics on the server — wait 2 minutes, after which respawn is allowed.</span>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Faction Rules Update">
    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-10"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>A. General Faction Rules</strong></h3></div>

    * **A-6.3** — <span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Removed point: "It is forbidden to use official functions (databases, wanted lists, calls, special signals, etc.) for personal purposes, trolling or pressuring players."</span> This norm has been merged into **A-7**.

    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-14"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>B. Multi-Faction</strong></h3></div>

    * **B-9** — Multi-faction prohibitions and permissions adjusted:
      * **<span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Removed from prohibited:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Government (IC, Prosecutor's Office, FSO) and any other faction.</span>
      * **<span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>Changed:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>OPG and Government (IC, Prosecutor's Office, FSO) → OPG and Government (IC, Prosecutor's Office, FSO, Court and others).</span>
      * **<span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>Removed from permitted combinations:</span>** <span style={{ color: '#ff0000' }}>FSO and Government (IC, Prosecutor's Office); FSO and MCHS; FSO and MZ; FSO and Department of Transport; FSO and FSVNG; FSO and MVD.</span>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="New ER:LC Staff Regulations">
    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-10"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>Staff Regulations</strong></h3></div>

    Completely new ER:LC staff regulation documents have been introduced. The following pages have been added:

    * [**ER:LC Staff Regulations**](/en/staff/staff-rules/erlc-staff-rules) — detailed regulations on duties, discipline, patrolling, and staff balance.
    * [**Curator Rules**](/en/staff/staff-rules/curator-rules) — norms and responsibilities of faction curators.
    * [**Promotion Team Rules**](/en/staff/staff-rules/pr-rules) — regulations for the PR team and media partners.
    * [**Staff Rights**](/en/staff/general/staff-rights) — basic employee rights.
    * [**Ethical Norms**](/en/staff/general/ethical-norms) — professional ethics standards.

    <div className="flex justify-center items-center w-full -mt-14"><h3 className="text-3xl font-bold"><strong>Main Changes</strong></h3></div>

    * <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>Staff rules have been **completely rewritten**: added responsibility for regulation violations, mandatory screen recording on shift, all punishments must be issued through the bot, and reporting for actions outside of shift.</span>
    * <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>A new discipline system has been introduced: warnings and reprimands up to automatic dismissal, with a mandatory review process for each incident.</span>
    * <span style={{ color: '#00be00' }}>Added staff balance norms and mandatory server patrolling depending on the online count.</span>
  </Accordion>
